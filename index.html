FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I create quotes?
After free registration you can start immediately. Describe your project in the generator — the AI creates the quote in no time. The Zera chat is available from the Starter plan upwards.
Are the quotes legally compliant?
Yes. All quotes automatically include the relevant legal texts under German civil law (BGB § 312g right of withdrawal, § 634a warranty) and professional payment terms.
How does the AI know my prices?
Initially, the AI uses market-rate prices for the DACH region. With every quote you save, it learns your individual prices and suggests them automatically next time.
Can I add my company logo?
Yes! Upload your logo once in the company profile. It automatically appears on every quote and from the Starter plan upwards also in the PDF export.
How much does GuruShareAI cost?
There is a free version to test the waters — it is limited to 10 quotes and 1 variation per month. For professional use, we offer various subscription plans starting at €29/month (e.g., Starter, Pro, Business). You can find all the details in the pricing section above.
Does it work for service providers too, not just tradespeople?
Absolutely! GuruShareAI works for all industries: trades, IT, consulting, creative agencies, cleaning, landscaping — anywhere quotes are written.
